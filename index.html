Meet the Pro Behind Piggyback Pros

Experience That Sets Us Apart

Founded by Thomas Colbert, Piggyback Pros brings over 11 years of unmatched experience in truck-mounted forklift operations—from the ground up, literally.

Thomas didn't start as a repair technician. He began his career building and customizing truck-mounted forklifts, fabricating systems from scratch and engineering solutions for demanding applications. This foundation gave him an understanding of these machines that most technicians will never achieve.

After years of building, Thomas transitioned to field service, spending years diagnosing failures, performing complex repairs, and keeping critical equipment operational for businesses across the region. He learned that downtime costs money, and customers need solutions—not excuses.

Most recently, Thomas served in service management, leading technical teams and preparing detailed estimates in SAP systems. This business perspective taught him the importance of accurate quoting, transparent communication, and delivering real value.
